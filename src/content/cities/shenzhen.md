Shenzhen, once a fishing village, is now China's tech capital — a futuristic city of glass towers, innovation, and theme parks.

## Highlights

- **Ping An Finance Centre** — 599 m, one of the world's tallest
- **OCT Loft** — creative arts district
- **Window of the World** — mini replicas of world landmarks
- **Dameisha Beach** — escape the city
- **Hong Kong border** — 30 minutes by metro

## Getting Around

The metro is modern, cheap, and English-signed. Shenzhen is the easiest city to enter from Hong Kong — take the MTR East Rail to Lo Wu or Lok Ma Chau, then connect to the metro.

## Where to Stay

- **Futian** — central, business district
- **Luohu** — near the Hong Kong border
- **Nanshan** — tech hub, coastal walks

## Food

Cantonese seafood, hotpot chains, and street food. Shenzhen is a food melting pot — you'll find every regional Chinese cuisine here.
