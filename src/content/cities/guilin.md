Guilin's karst peaks and the Li River form the landscape that defines China's postcard image — a dreamy world of misty mountains and emerald water.

## Highlights

- **Li River Cruise** — the classic 4-hour cruise to Yangshuo
- **Yangshuo** — cycling through karst countryside
- **Longji Rice Terraces** — dramatic hillside terraces
- **Reed Flute Cave** — illuminated limestone caverns
- **Elephant Trunk Hill** — Guilin's symbol

## Getting Around

The Li River cruise is the highlight — book through your hotel or a reputable agency. Yangshuo is best explored by bike or e-bike. High-speed rail connects Guilin to Guangzhou and Guiyang.

## Where to Stay

- **Guilin city** — base for the cruise
- **Yangshuo** — countryside charm, nightlife
- **Longji** — stay in a terrace-side guesthouse

## Food

Guilin rice noodles (the city's signature), beer fish in Yangshuo, and bamboo rice. Guangxi food is light and fresh.

## Local Insider Tips

- **Li River**: the 4-hour cruise from Guilin to Yangshuo is the classic, but the **bamboo rafts on the highlight stretch** (Xingping to Nine Horses Fresco Hill) cover the best scenery in a fraction of the time.
- **The ¥20 note** — turn the bill over and you are looking at Xingping (兴坪) wharf. Stand there and match the photo.
- **Yangshuo West Street** is where every foreign backpacker ends up, and it is now loud and commercial. The **Yulong River** (遇龙河) rafts, 20 minutes out, are the quiet version.
- **Ten-Mile Gallery** (十里画廊) by e-bike is ¥30–50 a day and the single best way to see the karst countryside. Bring a light rain jacket, not an umbrella.
- **Elephant Trunk Hill** (象鼻山) has been free since 2022 — the city's logo rock, 20 minutes, no need to linger.
- **Longji Rice Terraces**: flooded and mirror-like in May–June, gold in September–October. Stay one night in a terrace-side guesthouse — the sunrise is the whole point.
- **Guilin rice noodles** are ¥5–8: served dry with braised meat and peanuts first, then you add the bone broth yourself. Chongshan (崇善) and Shiji (石记) are the old shops.
- **Beer fish** in Yangshuo: pick a restaurant with clearly displayed prices. The fish is priced by weight — confirm before it goes in the pan.

## Practical Details

- **Weather and water**: September–November gives clear water and blue skies; April–May is the misty, painterly Li River — but rain can muddy the river.
- **Airport**: Liangjiang (KWL) is 30 km out; the airport bus is ¥20.
- **Rail**: Guilin North/West to Yangshuo station is 30 minutes, but the station is another 40 minutes from Yangshuo town — budget for the transfer.
- **Skip**: the "Shangri-La" theme park and the fake minority villages if time is short. Sanjie Liu (印象刘三姐) is ¥200+ and weather-dependent — spectacular when it runs.
- **Souvenirs**: the "three treasures" — Sanhua liquor, chilli sauce, fermented tofu — plus osmanthus cake.
- **Time needed**: 3 days (1 Guilin + 2 Yangshuo) is the baseline; a fourth day adds Longji.
