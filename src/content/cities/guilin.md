Guilin's karst peaks and the Li River form the landscape that defines China's postcard image — a dreamy world of misty mountains and emerald water.

## Highlights

- **Li River Cruise** — the classic 4-hour cruise to Yangshuo
- **Yangshuo** — cycling through karst countryside
- **Longji Rice Terraces** — dramatic hillside terraces
- **Reed Flute Cave** — illuminated limestone caverns
- **Elephant Trunk Hill** — Guilin's symbol

## Getting Around

The Li River cruise is the highlight — book through your hotel or a reputable agency. Yangshuo is best explored by bike or e-bike. High-speed rail connects Guilin to Guangzhou and Guiyang.

## Where to Stay

- **Guilin city** — base for the cruise
- **Yangshuo** — countryside charm, nightlife
- **Longji** — stay in a terrace-side guesthouse

## Food

Guilin rice noodles (the city's signature), beer fish in Yangshuo, and bamboo rice. Guangxi food is light and fresh.
