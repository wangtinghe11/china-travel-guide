Shanghai is China's most international city — a dazzling mix of colonial architecture, futuristic skyscrapers, and world-class food.

## Highlights

- **The Bund** — colonial waterfront, best at sunset
- **Lujiazui / Pudong** — Shanghai Tower, Oriental Pearl, skyline views
- **French Concession** — tree-lined streets, cafés, boutiques
- **Yu Garden** — classical Ming dynasty garden
- **Tianzifang** — artsy lanes and craft shops

## Local Insider Tips

- **Bund lights**: On in summer from 19:00, winter from 17:30. The skyline is most beautiful half an hour after dark.
- **Maglev train**: 8 minutes from Pudong Airport to Longyang Road — the world's fastest commercial train, worth trying once.
- **Disneyland**: Go on a weekday. Popular rides still queue 1–2 hours.
- **French Concession**: Wukang Road and Anfu Road are packed with cafés. Wukang Building is the iconic photo spot.
- **Benbang cuisine** (Shanghainese) is sweet — try **Lao Jishi (老吉士)** or **Lanxin (兰心)**, local favorites.
- **Shengjianbao**: One order is 4 pieces — don't over-order. **Da Huchun (大壶春)** is the traditional leavened-dough style.
- **Two airports**: Pudong and Hongqiao — don't mix them up! Hongqiao is much closer to the city center.
- **Weather**: Rainy season is mid-June to early July (hot and muggy). Winter is damp and feels colder than the temperature suggests.
- **Biking**: Renting a shared bike to ride through the French Concession is delightful.
- **Day trips**: High-speed rail reaches Suzhou and Hangzhou in 30 minutes — easy to add to your itinerary. Nanjing is about 1 hour.
- **Nanjing Road**: Packed with people, but the lights are pretty at night — the classic photo spot is in front of the Peace Hotel (和平饭店).
- **Suzhou Creek**: The waterfront walk from the Bund to the M50 art district is quiet and scenic — fewer crowds than the Bund promenade.
- **Zhujiajiao water town**: 1 hour from the city, less crowded than Zhouzhuang or Wuzhen — half a day is enough.
- **Nightlife**: Xintiandi and the Bund's Waitanyuan have plenty of bars; Hengshan Road is the classic bar street, open until 2 AM.
- **Shopping**: Nanjing West Road (Henglong Plaza, Jing'an Kerry Centre) is the luxury zone; Qipu Road (七浦路) is the wholesale market where you bargain.
- **Tourist SIM at PVG**: Carrier counters at Pudong Airport sell tourist SIM cards with your passport — cheaper than an eSIM but more hassle.

## Getting Around

The metro is excellent and English-signed. The Maglev train connects Pudong Airport to the city in 8 minutes. Ferries across the Huangpu River cost ¥2 and give great skyline views.

## Where to Stay

- **The Bund / People's Square** — central, classic
- **Former French Concession** — charming, cafés
- **Lujiazui** — modern, skyline views

## Food

Xiaolongbao (soup dumplings), shengjianbao (pan-fried buns), and hairy crab in autumn. Book Din Tai Fung or local favorites via Dianping.
