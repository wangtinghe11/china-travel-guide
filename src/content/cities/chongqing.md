Chongqing is a surreal mountain city where buildings stack on top of each other, the metro dives underground, and hotpot is a way of life.

## Highlights

- **Hongya Cave** — 11-story stilted complex, magical at night
- **Jiefangbei** — the city's shopping heart
- **Yangtze River Cableway** — cross the river like a local
- **Liziba Station** — metro passing through a building
- **Wulong Karst** — day trip, natural bridges and caves

## Getting Around

The metro is the only sane way to navigate this vertical city — GPS is unreliable here. The famous Liziba station (train through a building) is on Line 2.

## Where to Stay

- **Jiefangbei** — central, nightlife
- **Nan'an** — skyline views across the river
- **Guanyinqiao** — shopping district

## Food

Chongqing hotpot is the spiciest in China — order the "foreigner mild" broth if unsure. Try xiaomian (small noodles) for breakfast.

## Local Insider Tips

- **Hongya Cave**: the view everyone photographs is *from* the Qiansimen Bridge (千厮门大桥), not from inside the complex. Go at dusk, shoot from the bridge, then walk down through it.
- **Liziba Station** (李子坝) — the metro running through a residential block — is a sight in itself. Ride Line 2 through it rather than standing on the viewing platform with the crowd.
- **GPS lies here.** Chongqing is built on ridges, so a two-minute walk on the map can mean six flights of stairs. Wear real shoes, and trust street signs over your phone.
- **Yangtze River Cableway**: expect an hour in the queue for a five-minute crossing. Go at dusk for the lights, and take it one way only — walk back across the bridge.
- **Hotpot here is nine-grid with beef tallow** (九宫格牛油锅). "Mild" in Chongqing equals "medium" anywhere else. The centre grid is the hottest — keep to the outer rings.
- **Xiaomian** (小面): ¥7–10 for a two-liang bowl, eaten standing at 7 a.m. like everyone else. Order 豌杂面 (pea and minced pork) for the classic.
- **Night view**: Nanshan Yikeshu (南山一棵树) looks over the whole peninsula, or take a two-river cruise. The cableway at dusk is the cheap version of the same view.
- **Baixiangju** (白象居) — the old residential block where *Better Days* was shot — is the best free vantage point for cable cars crossing between towers.
- **Yellow taxis** ("yellow Ferraris") are famous for a reason: the drivers treat the mountain roads like a rally stage. Skip the front seat if you get carsick.
- **Suanlafen** (酸辣粉) at Haoyoulai by Jiefangbei, ¥10 a bowl, is the standard-issue local lunch.

## Practical Details

- **Airport**: Jiangbei (CKG) into the city on Metro Line 10, about 40 minutes.
- **Weather**: fog city. Summer is a 40°C furnace, winter is damp and cold — May and October are the sweet spots.
- **Where to stay**: Jiefangbei or Hongya Cave for the view and the walkability. Beware "river-view" rooms whose view is a tree.
- **Time needed**: 2 days for the city and the night skyline; add a third for Wulong's karst bridges (2.5 hours each way).
