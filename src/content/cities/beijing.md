Beijing is China's political and cultural heart — a city of imperial palaces, ancient hutongs, and futuristic architecture. Most first-time visitors to China start here.

## Highlights

- **Forbidden City** — the world's largest palace complex, book tickets online in advance
- **Great Wall** — Mutianyu (restored, cable car) or Jinshanling (wild, hiking)
- **Temple of Heaven** — morning tai chi in the park
- **Hutongs** — old alleyways around Shichahai and Nanluoguxiang
- **Summer Palace** — imperial gardens by the lake

## Getting Around

The Beijing metro is cheap, fast, and English-signed. Buy a transit card or use Alipay's transport QR code. Taxis are metered; Didi (Chinese Uber) is the most reliable way to hail one.

## Where to Stay

- **Wangfujing / Dongcheng** — central, near Forbidden City
- **Sanlitun** — nightlife and dining
- **Qianmen** — traditional atmosphere near Tiananmen

## Food

Peking duck (Quanjude or Siji Minfu), zhajiangmian (noodles with soybean paste), and jianbing (savory crepes) from street stalls. Most restaurants accept Alipay/WeChat Pay; carry some cash as backup.
