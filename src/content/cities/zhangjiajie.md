Zhangjiajie's towering sandstone pillars inspired the floating mountains of Avatar — and the real thing is even more dramatic.

## Highlights

- **Zhangjiajie National Forest Park** — the Avatar pillars
- **Tianmen Mountain** — cable car, glass walkway, heaven's gate
- **Zhangjiajie Grand Canyon Glass Bridge** — the world's longest
- **Yuanjiajie** — the "Avatar Hallelujah Mountain"
- **Bailong Elevator** — 326 m glass elevator in the cliff

## Getting Around

The park is huge — plan 2-3 days. Use the park's shuttle buses between scenic areas. Tianmen Mountain is a separate ticket from the forest park. High-speed rail connects Zhangjiajie to Changsha and Guangzhou.

## Where to Stay

- **Wulingyuan** — the gateway town to the forest park
- **Zhangjiajie city** — near the station and Tianmen
- **In-park guesthouses** — for sunrise views

## Food

Hunan food is famously spicy — try sanxiaguo (three-in-one pot) and tujia bacon. Ask for "not spicy" if you're sensitive.

## Local Insider Tips

- **The Avatar mountain is real and specific**: the Hallelujah Floating Mountain is the **Qiankun Pillar** in **Yuanjiajie** (袁家界). Everyone photographs it from the same platform — go early or late.
- **National Forest Park ticket is ¥228 and valid for four days.** The core is Yuanjiajie, Tianzi Mountain and the Golden Whip Stream — don't try to do all three in one day.
- **Tianmen Mountain** is a separate ticket and a separate day: the 7.5 km cableway (one of the world's longest), the cliff-hugging glass walkway, and the 99-bend road up to Heaven's Gate.
- **Glass Bridge** over the Grand Canyon is the highest and longest of its kind — genuinely not for anyone afraid of heights, and phones go in a pouch so they can't be dropped.
- **Golden Whip Stream** (金鞭溪) is a 7.5 km valley walk, and the monkeys are organised thieves: they will take food, glasses and open bags. Zip everything.
- **Bailong Elevator** — 326 m up the cliff face in about a minute. Take it up, walk down; the queue going up is shorter in the afternoon.
- **Stay in Wulingyuan**, at the park gate, not in Zhangjiajie city — the city is 40 minutes away and you lose the early hours.
- **Sanxiaguo** (三下锅) is the Tujia specialty — a dry pot of three meats. Eat it in the town, not inside the park where prices double.
- **Fenghuang ancient town** (凤凰古城) is an hour by high-speed rail and worth the detour for the stilt houses over the Tuo River at night — commercial, but beautiful.

## Practical Details

- **Weather and light**: best after rain, when the pillars sit in cloud — that's the "ink painting" shot. April–June is misty, September–November is clear and golden. Avoid the October golden week entirely.
- **Airport and rail**: Hehua (DYG) is 10 minutes from the city but has few flights; many travellers fly to Changsha and take the 3-hour high-speed train. Zhangjiajie West station connects to Changsha and Guangzhou.
- **Effort**: expect 20,000 steps a day. Hiking shoes, not trainers. Take every cable car and elevator you can — the walking is still substantial.
- **Time needed**: 2 days (forest park + Tianmen), 3 with the glass bridge, 4 with Fenghuang.
