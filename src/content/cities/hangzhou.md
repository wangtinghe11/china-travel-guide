Hangzhou, with its misty West Lake and rolling tea hills, has been China's most romantic city for a thousand years.

## Highlights

- **West Lake** — boat rides, causeways, and sunset views
- **Longjing Tea Village** — watch tea picking and roasting
- **Lingyin Temple** — one of China's great Buddhist temples
- **Grand Canal** — UNESCO heritage, evening cruises
- **Alibaba HQ** — the tech giant's campus (view from outside)

## Getting Around

The metro covers the main sights. West Lake is best explored on foot, by bike, or by boat. Longjing village is a short bus or Didi ride from the lake.

## Where to Stay

- **West Lake area** — the classic choice
- **Wulin Square** — shopping and dining
- **Heming Road** — boutique hotels

## Food

Dongpo pork, West Lake vinegar fish, and Longjing shrimp. Hangzhou cuisine is refined and delicate — a contrast to Sichuan's fire.
