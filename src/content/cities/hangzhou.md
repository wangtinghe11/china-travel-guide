Hangzhou, with its misty West Lake and rolling tea hills, has been China's most romantic city for a thousand years.

## Highlights

- **West Lake** — boat rides, causeways, and sunset views
- **Longjing Tea Village** — watch tea picking and roasting
- **Lingyin Temple** — one of China's great Buddhist temples
- **Grand Canal** — UNESCO heritage, evening cruises
- **Alibaba HQ** — the tech giant's campus (view from outside)

## Getting Around

The metro covers the main sights. West Lake is best explored on foot, by bike, or by boat. Longjing village is a short bus or Didi ride from the lake.

## Where to Stay

- **West Lake area** — the classic choice
- **Wulin Square** — shopping and dining
- **Heming Road** — boutique hotels

## Food

Dongpo pork, West Lake vinegar fish, and Longjing shrimp. Hangzhou cuisine is refined and delicate — a contrast to Sichuan's fire.

## Local Insider Tips

- **West Lake is free** and the 15 km loop is best done by bike — early morning, before the tour buses, is a completely different lake.
- **Lingyin Temple** (灵隐寺) is ¥75 combined with the Feilai Peak grottoes. Be there before 7 a.m.: the incense smoke, the chanting and no crowd is the actual experience.
- **Longjing tea village**: ¥50–100 to sit and drink tea picked that morning. Do **not** buy loose-leaf tea from a scenic-area stall — buy from the farmer you sat with, or skip it.
- **Hand-rowed boats** (手摇船) on the lake are ~¥150 an hour and far better than the big ferries — the boatman will take you into the quiet western inlets.
- **Food**: West Lake vinegar fish and Dongpo pork are the famous dishes, but locals eat at **Grandma's Home (外婆家)** and **Green Tea (绿茶)** for the value. **Pian'er chuan** (片儿川, ¥15–20) is the breakfast noodle.
- **Xiaolongbao here uses leavened dough** — thicker and fluffier than Shanghai's. Zhiweiguan (知味观) and Xinfeng (新丰) are the old names.
- **Sept–October** the whole city smells of osmanthus. Spring and autumn are the two seasons worth planning around; summer is 35°C+ and winter is a damp cold that gets into your bones.
- **Jiuxi (九溪烟树)** is the local weekend walk — stream, tea terraces, maples in autumn. Almost no foreign visitors.
- **Songcheng show** (宋城千古情) is genuinely spectacular staging, at ¥300. Skip it if the budget is tight.
- **Qianjiang New City light show** runs at 19:30 and 20:30; the free view is from City Balcony (城市阳台) on the river.
- **China Silk Museum** is free; buy silk scarves at the Silk City market and bargain.

## Practical Details

- **Airport**: Xiaoshan (HGH) — Metro Line 19 into the city in about 40 minutes.
- **Cashless**: Hangzhou is Alipay's home city. Some stalls genuinely cannot make change for cash.
- **Time needed**: 2 days for the lake and Lingyin; add Xixi wetland or Jiuxi for a third.
