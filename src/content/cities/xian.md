Xi'an, the ancient capital of 13 dynasties, is home to the Terracotta Army and the best-preserved city wall in China.

## Highlights

- **Terracotta Army** — 8,000 life-size warriors, 1 hour from the city
- **City Wall** — cycle the 14 km perimeter at sunset
- **Muslim Quarter** — street food paradise
- **Big Wild Goose Pagoda** — Tang dynasty landmark
- **Shaanxi History Museum** — free but book ahead

## Getting Around

The metro covers the main sights. The Terracotta Army is easiest by tourist bus 5 (306) or a Didi ride (~¥150). City wall bikes rent at the gates.

## Where to Stay

- **Bell Tower area** — central, walkable
- **Muslim Quarter** — food at your doorstep
- **Qujiang** — modern, near the pagoda

## Food

Biangbiang noodles, roujiamo (Chinese burger), and yangrou paomo (lamb soup with bread). The Muslim Quarter is the place to graze.
