# Best eSIM for China 2026: Stay Connected Without a Physical SIM

China is one of the few countries where your phone setup matters as much as your itinerary. Google, WhatsApp, Instagram, and Facebook are blocked — but with the right eSIM (and a VPN), you'll stay connected the whole trip.

## Why You Need an eSIM for China

- **Instant setup** — install before you fly, activate on landing
- **No physical SIM** — no hunting for a China Mobile shop
- **Keep your home number** — for banking OTPs and 2FA
- **Data-only plans** — perfect for maps, translation, and messaging

## The Great Firewall: What's Blocked

- **Blocked**: Google (all services), Gmail, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, X/Twitter, YouTube, Netflix, most Western news sites
- **Works**: Apple services (mostly), Microsoft, LinkedIn (sometimes), most Chinese apps (WeChat, Alipay, Didi, Baidu Maps)
- **Key workaround**: A VPN or an eSIM plan with built-in VPN

## Best eSIMs for China (2026)

### 1. Airalo — Best Overall Value
- **Plans**: 1 GB to 20 GB, 7–30 days
- **Price**: From ~$4.50 (1 GB/7 days) to ~$26 (20 GB/30 days)
- **Coverage**: China Unicom / China Mobile
- **Pros**: Cheap, easy app, instant activation
- **Cons**: No built-in VPN — you'll need a separate VPN app

### 2. Holafly — Best for Unlimited Data
- **Plans**: Unlimited data, 5–90 days
- **Price**: From ~$19 (5 days) to ~$99 (90 days)
- **Coverage**: China Unicom
- **Pros**: Unlimited data, 24/7 support
- **Cons**: Pricier, no built-in VPN

### 3. Nomad — Best for Flexible Top-Ups
- **Plans**: Pay-as-you-go, 1 GB to 20 GB
- **Price**: From ~$3.50
- **Pros**: Flexible, good app
- **Cons**: No built-in VPN

### 4. China Mobile Official eSIM (via Trip.com)
- **Plans**: 7–30 days, data + local number
- **Price**: From ~$15
- **Pros**: Local number (useful for Didi and food delivery), official carrier
- **Cons**: More setup, still no VPN

## The VPN Question

**Your eSIM does NOT bypass the Great Firewall.** Data flows through Chinese networks either way. You need:

1. **A VPN app** (ExpressVPN, NordVPN, Astrill, or a China-friendly one like LetsVPN) — install and test BEFORE you fly
2. **Or a travel router** with pre-configured VPN (overkill for most)

**Pro tip**: Download your VPN app, log in, and test it at home. Many VPNs get blocked in China — the ones that work change constantly. Astrill and LetsVPN are the most reliable in China as of 2026.

## What About Google Maps?

**Google Maps is blocked and unreliable in China.** Use these instead:

- **Apple Maps** — works with Chinese data (uses local map data)
- **Amap (高德地图)** — the Chinese Google Maps, has English mode
- **Baidu Maps** — similar, more Chinese
- **Metro apps** — each city's metro app has English route planning

**Pro tip**: Download offline maps of your cities before you fly.

## Setup Checklist

- [ ] Check your phone supports eSIM (iPhone XS+, recent Androids)
- [ ] Buy an eSIM plan (Airalo/Holafly/Nomad) — install before flying
- [ ] Install and test a VPN app at home
- [ ] Download offline maps (Apple Maps or Amap)
- [ ] Download WeChat, Alipay, and Didi before you land
- [ ] Save your hotel address in Chinese characters (show the taxi driver)
