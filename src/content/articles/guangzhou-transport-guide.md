# How to Get Around Guangzhou: Metro, Taxis & Day Trips

**The short answer**: take the metro. Guangzhou's metro is excellent, cheap, and English-signed — it covers all the major sights and goes straight to Chimelong. Budget ¥2–8 per ride.

## The Metro: Excellent & English-Friendly

Guangzhou's metro is one of the best in China — clean, fast, and with English signs and announcements on every line.

- **Lines you'll use most**: Line 1 (Beijing Road, Chen Clan Ancestral Hall), Line 2 (Baiyun Airport, Guangzhou South), Line 3 (Canton Tower, Chimelong), Line 5 (Zhujiang New Town).
- **Payment**: Scan a QR code with Alipay or WeChat Pay at the gates — no ticket needed.
- **Fares**: ¥2–8 per ride.
- **Peak hours**: 7:30–9:30 AM and 5:30–7:30 PM — the trains get packed.

## Taxis & Didi

- **Flag-fall**: About ¥12.
- **Didi**: Works well — set your pickup point carefully.
- **Tip**: The metro is almost always faster in rush hour.

## Airport Transfer: Baiyun Airport (CAN)

- **Metro Line 3**: From Baiyun Airport to the city center in about 40 minutes — the best option.
- **Taxi/Didi**: 40–60 minutes depending on traffic, ¥80–120.
- **Tip**: Check which terminal you're flying into — the metro serves both.

## Day Trips: The Pearl River Delta Hub

Guangzhou is the transport hub of southern China — perfect for day trips:

- **Foshan (30 min by high-speed rail)**: The Ancestral Temple (祖庙) and Lingnan Tiandi (岭南天地) — martial arts heritage and old streets.
- **Zhuhai (1 hour)**: Coastal city, home of the Hong Kong–Zhuhai–Macau Bridge.
- **Shenzhen (1 hour)**: China's youngest megacity — tech markets and seaside parks.
- **Hong Kong (via high-speed rail, ~1 hour)**: The West Kowloon station is a direct connection.

## The Pearl River Night Cruise (珠江夜游)

A lovely way to see the city lights: ¥80–150 for about an hour, departing from **Tianzi Wharf (天字码头)** or **Dashatou (大沙头)**. Book in the evening for the best views of the Canton Tower.

## Walking & Cycling

- **Shamian Island (沙面)**: A quiet, leafy enclave of European colonial buildings — perfect for walking.
- **Beijing Road (北京路)**: A pedestrian shopping street — walkable and lively.
- **Bike-sharing**: Shared bikes are available, but the summer heat makes cycling less pleasant.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Summer heat**: The metro is air-conditioned — a relief from the 40°C summer humidity.
- **Sudden rainstorms**: Carry an umbrella — Guangzhou's summer downpours come without warning.
- **Wholesale markets**: Shisanhang (十三行) and Baima (白马) close in the afternoon — go in the morning.

## FAQ

**What is the best way to get around Guangzhou?** The metro — excellent, cheap, and English-signed.

**How do I get from Baiyun Airport to the city?** Metro Line 3, about 40 minutes.

**Can I do a day trip to Foshan?** Yes — 30 minutes by high-speed rail. Zhuhai and Shenzhen are about 1 hour away.

**Is the Pearl River night cruise worth it?** Yes — ¥80–150 for an hour, from Tianzi Wharf or Dashatou.

**Are taxis expensive?** No — cheap, but the metro is faster in rush hour.
