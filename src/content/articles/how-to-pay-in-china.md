# How to Pay in China 2026: Alipay, WeChat Pay & Cash for Foreigners

China is the world's most cashless society. Street vendors, taxis, museums, and even temple donation boxes accept QR code payments. Here's how to pay as a foreigner.

## The Short Answer

**Set up Alipay before you fly.** It's the single most useful app for foreign travelers in China. WeChat Pay is a good backup. Carry a little cash for emergencies.

## Setting Up Alipay (10 Minutes)

1. **Download Alipay** from your app store
2. **Register** with your foreign phone number and email
3. **Verify your identity** — passport photo + selfie (required for international cards)
4. **Link your card** — Visa, Mastercard, or JCB (international cards work since 2023)
5. **You're done** — scan QR codes to pay, or show your payment QR code

**Tips**:
- Alipay has a **Tour Pass** feature — preload money from your foreign card for a fixed exchange rate
- The app has an **English interface** — switch it in settings
- **Transaction limits** apply to foreign cards (usually ~$500/day for unverified, higher after verification)

## Setting Up WeChat Pay

WeChat Pay works similarly but is more finicky for foreigners:

1. Download WeChat, register with your phone number
2. Go to **Me → Services → Wallet**
3. Verify with your passport
4. Link your international card

**Note**: WeChat Pay's foreign-card support is more limited than Alipay's. If you can only set up one, choose Alipay.

## When You Still Need Cash

- **Small street vendors** and night markets (some don't take cards)
- **Rural areas** and small towns
- **Tipping** (not customary, but small change helps)
- **Backup** if your phone dies or card gets blocked

**Getting cash**: ATMs at Bank of China, ICBC, and other major banks accept Visa/Mastercard. Withdraw ¥500–¥1000 at a time. Some ATMs charge a fee — check your bank's international policy.

## Paying at Restaurants & Shops

- **Scan to pay**: The merchant shows a QR code; you scan it in Alipay/WeChat and enter the amount
- **Show your code**: You show your payment QR code; the merchant scans it
- **Split bills**: Just have each person pay separately — no awkward splitting needed
- **Tipping**: Not expected in China. Rounding up is fine.

## Common Pitfalls

- ❌ **Assuming your foreign card works everywhere** — it doesn't. QR payments are king.
- ❌ **Not verifying your identity** before arrival — do it at home with good Wi-Fi
- ❌ **Forgetting your passport** for hotel check-in and some ticket purchases
- ❌ **Relying on one payment method** — have Alipay + cash as backup

## Quick Setup Checklist

- [ ] Download Alipay and register with your foreign number
- [ ] Verify identity with passport (do this before you fly)
- [ ] Link your Visa/Mastercard
- [ ] Download WeChat and set up WeChat Pay as backup
- [ ] Withdraw ¥500 cash at the airport ATM
