# Xi'an Travel Guide 2026: Itineraries, Transport & Insider Tips

Xi'an, the ancient capital of 13 dynasties, is where China's history comes alive — the Terracotta Army, the best-preserved city wall in the country, and a food scene that rivals Chengdu. Here's how to do it right.

## Day 1: The Terracotta Army

**Morning**: Take the tourist bus (Line 5/306) or a Didi (~¥150) to the **Terracotta Army** (1 hour). Book tickets on the official site in advance and **hire a guide at the entrance** — without one, you're just looking at 8,000 clay soldiers without the story. Allow 2–3 hours across the three pits.

**Afternoon**: Back to the city → **City Wall** — rent a bike and cycle the full 13.7 km perimeter. Sunset from the wall is the most beautiful moment of the day.

**Evening**: **Muslim Quarter** street food — but skip the main strip. Locals eat at **Sajin Bridge (洒金桥)** and **Da Piyuan (大皮院)**: roujiamo (Chinese burger), yangrou paomo (lamb soup with bread), and cold noodles.

## Day 2: Ancient & Modern

**Morning**: **Shaanxi History Museum** (free but extremely hard to book — reserve weeks ahead; if you can't get in, the **Xi'an Museum** is a great backup) → **Big Wild Goose Pagoda** (Tang dynasty landmark, climb for city views).

**Afternoon**: Rent a **hanfu (traditional dress)** — ¥100–300 for a full outfit and photos, hugely popular and very photogenic around the pagoda and city wall.

**Evening**: **Datang Everbright City (大唐不夜城)** — free, with a spectacular light show at night. It gets packed — go early evening and enjoy the Tang-dynasty street performances.

## Day 3 (Optional): Mount Hua

**Mount Hua** (华山) is 2 hours by high-speed rail — famous for the terrifying **Plank Walk in the Sky (长空栈道)**. If you're not fit, take the cable car up and down. A full day trip; the mountain is stunning but demanding.

## More Hidden Gems

- **Shuyuanmen (书院门)**: A quiet street of calligraphy, painting, and brush shops — far calmer than the Muslim Quarter and a lovely place to browse.
- **Yongxingfang (永兴坊)**: The birthplace of the bowl-smashing wine (摔碗酒) trend — cleaner than the Muslim Quarter's main strip, and locals actually go here too.
- **Forest of Stone Steles Museum (碑林)**: A calligraphy sanctuary with original works by Yan Zhenqing and Liu Gongquan — ¥65, and much quieter than the big sights.

## Where to Eat Like a Local

- **Biangbiang noodles & youpo noodles (油泼面)**: Any small local joint beats the tourist spots — the simpler the shop, the better the noodles.
- **Roujiamo**: The "Chinese burger" — crispy flatbread stuffed with braised pork. Grab one from a street stall.
- **Yangrou paomo**: Lamb soup with torn flatbread — a Xi'an institution. You tear the bread yourself, the kitchen cooks it with the broth.
- **Hanfu photo spots**: The city wall and Big Wild Goose Pagoda are the best backdrops.

## Practical Tips

- **Tickets**: Book the Terracotta Army and Shaanxi History Museum online with your passport in advance. The museum is free but the hardest ticket in town.
- **Metro**: Line 14 connects Xianyang Airport (40 km out) to downtown. The metro covers all major sights.
- **Airport bus**: The airport shuttle from Xianyang Airport costs ¥25 — a budget alternative to the metro or a Didi.
- **Taxis**: The flag-fall is only ¥8.5, so taxis are cheap — but traffic jams at peak hours are brutal. Metro first, taxi second.
- **City Wall**: Bikes rent at the gates — 13.7 km takes about 1.5–2 hours with stops. Sunset is the golden hour.
- **Weather**: Spring and autumn are best. Summer is hot and dry (38°C+); spring can bring sandstorms. Winter is cold and dry.
- **Language**: English is limited — have a translation app ready.

## Where to Stay

- **Bell Tower area** — central, walkable to everything
- **Muslim Quarter** — food at your doorstep
- **Qujiang** — modern, near the Big Wild Goose Pagoda
