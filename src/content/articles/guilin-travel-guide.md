# Guilin Travel Guide 2026: Itineraries, Transport & Insider Tips

Guilin is the landscape that made China famous — karst peaks rising from misty rivers, the scene on the 20-yuan note, and rice terraces that climb into the clouds. Here's the complete guide.

## Day 1: Guilin City

**Morning**: **Elephant Trunk Hill (象鼻山)** — free since 2022, the symbol of Guilin. The classic photo is the elephant drinking from the river.

**Afternoon**: **Reed Flute Cave (芦笛岩)** — a spectacular limestone cave of stalactites and colored lights. Or walk the **Two Rivers and Four Lakes (两江四湖)** area.

**Evening**: **Guilin rice noodles (桂林米粉)** — ¥5–8 a bowl, and the proper way is **dry-mixed first, then add broth**. **Chongshan (崇善)** and **Shiji (石记)** are the local institutions.

## Day 2: The Li River to Yangshuo

**Morning**: **Li River cruise (漓江游船)** — 4 hours from Guilin to Yangshuo, the classic way to see the karst peaks. Or take a **bamboo raft on the essence section (精华段)** — quieter and more intimate.

**Afternoon**: Arrive in **Yangshuo** — check in, then rent an **e-bike (¥30–50/day)** and ride the **Ten-Mile Gallery (十里画廊)**. The countryside is the real attraction.

**Evening**: **West Street (西街)** — Yangshuo's famous foreigner strip, full of bars and restaurants. For a quieter night, head to the **Yulong River (遇龙河)** area.

## Day 3: Yulong River & Xingping

**Morning**: **Yulong River bamboo raft (遇龙河竹筏)** — the quieter, more beautiful alternative to the Li River. Drift past rice paddies and karst peaks.

**Afternoon**: **Xingping Ancient Town (兴坪古镇)** — the **20-yuan note view** is at the pier. Bring a 20-yuan note and hold it up against the real landscape. Then take the bus or train back to Guilin.

## Day 4 (Optional): Longji Rice Terraces

**Longji Rice Terraces (龙脊梯田)** — 2 hours from Guilin. **May–June** the terraces are flooded (mirror effect); **September–October** is the golden harvest. **Stay overnight** in a village guesthouse to catch the sunrise — it's worth it.

## Where to Eat Like a Local

- **Guilin rice noodles (桂林米粉)**: ¥5–8, dry-mixed first then add broth. Chongshan and Shiji are the classics.
- **Beer fish (啤酒鱼)**: Yangshuo's signature dish — fish braised in beer. Choose a restaurant with clear prices on the menu.
- **Longji bamboo rice**: Sticky rice cooked in bamboo tubes at the terraces.
- **Guilin three treasures (桂林三宝)**: Sanhua wine (三花酒), chili sauce (辣椒酱), and fermented bean curd (豆腐乳) — plus osmanthus cakes (桂花糕) as souvenirs.

## Practical Tips

- **Best time**: September–November for clear river water; April–May for the misty "smoke-rain Li River" atmosphere. The river is muddier in the June–August rainy season.
- **Getting around**: Buses and trains connect Guilin, Yangshuo, and Xingping. E-bikes are the way to explore the countryside.
- **Airport**: Liangjiang Airport (KWL) is 30 km from the city — airport bus ¥20. Guilin North/West stations to Yangshuo station: 30 minutes by train, then 40 minutes by car to the town.
- **Money**: Cash is still useful in small villages — carry some.
- **Weather**: Humid and rainy — pack a light rain jacket year-round.

## Where to Stay

- **Guilin city** — near the Two Rivers and Four Lakes
- **Yangshuo West Street** — lively, touristy
- **Yulong River / countryside** — quiet, beautiful guesthouses
