# Hangzhou Travel Guide 2026: Itineraries, Transport & Insider Tips

Hangzhou is the city of poets — West Lake, misty hills, and the birthplace of Longjing tea. It's the most beautiful city in eastern China, and the model for the phrase "heaven on earth." Here's the complete guide.

## Day 1: West Lake

**Morning**: **West Lake (西湖)** — free, and best experienced by **bike**. The 15 km loop around the lake is the classic ride; go early (before 8 AM) when the mist is on the water and the crowds haven't arrived. Stop at **Su Causeway (苏堤)** and **Bai Causeway (白堤)**.

**Afternoon**: **Hand-rowed boat (手摇船)** on the lake — ¥150/hour, far more atmospheric than the big tourist boats. The boatman will take you through the lotus and under the bridges.

**Evening**: **He Fang Street (河坊街)** — the old street with snacks and souvenirs. Or watch the sunset from **Baoshi Hill (宝石山)** overlooking the lake. After dark, the **Qianjiang New City light show (钱江新城灯光秀)** runs at 19:30 and 20:30 — free to watch from the City Balcony (城市阳台).

## Day 2: Tea & Temples

**Morning**: **Lingyin Temple (灵隐寺)** — ¥75 entry, one of China's great Buddhist temples. Go before 7 AM to beat the crowds and see the monks' morning routine. The **Feilai Feng (飞来峰)** grottoes are right next door.

**Afternoon**: **Longjing Village (龙井村)** — the home of Longjing tea. Sit in a teahouse and pay **¥50–100 for a cup of fresh Mingqian (pre-Qingming) tea**. Don't buy loose tea at the scenic spots — it's overpriced and often not the real thing.

**Evening**: Try **Pian'erchuan (片儿川)** — a ¥15–20 bowl of bamboo-shoot noodles, the local breakfast staple. For dinner, locals prefer **Waipo Jia (外婆家)** or **Lücha (绿茶)** over the tourist restaurants.

## Day 3 (Optional): Xixi or Jiuxi

**Xixi Wetland (西溪湿地)** — a peaceful water town of canals and reeds, best by boat. Or hike the **Jiuxi (九溪)** trail — a stream-and-forest path through tea fields and maples that's stunning in autumn, a favorite weekend escape for locals.

## Where to Eat Like a Local

- **Pian'erchuan (片儿川)**: Bamboo-shoot noodles — the local breakfast.
- **Xiaolongbao (小笼包)**: Hangzhou's version has a leavened (发面) wrapper, different from Shanghai's — try the old shops **Zhiweiguan (知味观)** or **Xinfeng (新丰)**.
- **West Lake vinegar fish (西湖醋鱼) & Dongpo pork (东坡肉)**: The famous Hangzhou dishes — try them at Waipo Jia or Lücha, where locals actually eat.
- **Longjing tea**: Fresh from the village, not the scenic spots.
- **Silk Museum (中国丝绸博物馆)**: Free entry — and if you want to buy silk scarves, head to the Silk City market (丝绸城) and bargain.

## Practical Tips

- **West Lake**: Free, bike-friendly, and best at dawn. The 15 km loop takes 1.5–2 hours with stops.
- **Lingyin Temple**: ¥75, go before 7 AM. The grottoes at Feilai Feng are included.
- **Alipay**: Hangzhou is Alipay's home city — the whole city is cashless. Make sure your Alipay is set up.
- **Weather**: Spring and autumn are best. Summer is hot (35°C+), winter is damp and cold. September–October brings osmanthus blossoms — the whole city smells incredible.
- **Day trips**: High-speed rail reaches Shanghai in 45 minutes — easy to combine.

## Where to Stay

- **West Lake area** — the classic choice, walkable to everything
- **Wulin Square** — central, shopping
- **Longjing Village** — tea-house guesthouses, countryside feel
