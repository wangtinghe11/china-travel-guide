# How to Get Around Beijing: Metro, Airports & Taxis

**The short answer**: take the metro — it's cheap, fast, and English-signed. Pay with Alipay's transport QR code or an NFC transit card. For airport runs, the express train beats taxis; if you do take a car, use Didi for transparent pricing.

## The Metro: Your Best Friend

Beijing's metro is one of the world's largest — 20+ lines covering every major sight. It's cheap, fast, and English-signed.

- **Lines you'll use most**: Line 1 (Forbidden City, Wangfujing), Line 2 (loop line — Qianmen, Yonghegong), Line 4 (Summer Palace, Zhongguancun), Line 8 (Olympic Park — Bird's Nest, Water Cube).
- **Payment**: Scan Alipay's or WeChat Pay's transport QR code at the gates — no ticket needed. Or use a **transit card** (公交地铁一卡通): one card works on buses and metro, and **phone NFC** can also be used.
- **Fares**: ¥3–9 per ride, depending on distance.
- **Peak hours**: 7:30–9:30 AM and 5:30–7:30 PM — packed. Avoid if you can.

## Airports: Don't Book the Wrong One

Beijing has two airports — and they're on opposite sides of the city.

- **Capital Airport (PEK)**: Close to the city — 25 minutes on the airport express train to Dongzhimen. Most international flights land here.
- **Daxing Airport (PKX)**: Far south, over an hour from the city center. Check your arrival airport before booking a hotel!
- **Airport express**: From Capital, about ¥25 to Dongzhimen. From Daxing, take the Daxing Airport Express to Caoqiao then transfer to the metro.

## Taxis & Didi

- **Taxis**: Metered and reasonable for short trips. Airport runs cost **¥100–150**.
- **Didi (滴滴)**: The ride-hailing app shows the fare upfront — **more transparent than a taxi**, especially for airport transfers. Set your pickup point carefully.
- **Tip**: In rush hour, the metro is almost always faster than a car.

## Buses & Bikes

- **Buses**: Cheap (¥1–2) and cover everywhere, but slower and harder to navigate without Chinese. The transit card works here too.
- **Shared bikes**: Beijing is flat and bike-friendly — a great way to explore hutongs. Scan with Alipay or WeChat.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Wrong airport**: PEK vs PKX — they're 60+ km apart. Always check which one your flight uses.
- **Rush hour**: The metro is brutally packed at peak times. Travel off-peak if you can.
- **Tiananmen security**: Expect passport checks and queues around Tiananmen Square — factor in extra time.

## FAQ

**Which airport should I fly into?** Capital (PEK) — much closer. Daxing (PKX) is far south.

**How do I get from Capital Airport to the city?** Airport express, ~25 minutes. Taxi/Didi ¥100–150.

**How do I pay for the metro?** Alipay/WeChat transport QR code, or a transit card (NFC works too).

**Are taxis expensive?** Short trips are fine; airport runs ¥100–150. Didi is more transparent.

**How do I avoid rush hour?** Avoid 7:30–9:30 AM and 5:30–7:30 PM on the metro.
