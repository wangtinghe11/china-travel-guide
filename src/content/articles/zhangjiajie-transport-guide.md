# How to Get Around Zhangjiajie: Getting There, Park Transport & Fenghuang

**The short answer**: fly into Hehua Airport if you can find a flight, otherwise fly to Changsha and take the 3-hour high-speed rail. Stay in Wulingyuan (武陵源) — right at the park entrance — and use the park's shuttle buses and cable cars to save energy. Budget ¥50–100 for transport per day.

## Getting to Zhangjiajie

- **Hehua Airport (DYG)**: 10 minutes from the city — but it has few flights. Check if your route is served before booking.
- **High-speed rail**: The most reliable option. **Zhangjiajie West Station** connects to:
  - **Changsha** (3 hours) — the main hub, with flights from everywhere.
  - **Fenghuang** (1 hour) — the ancient town side trip.
  - **Guangzhou, Shenzhen, and other cities** on the network.
- **Tip**: Many visitors fly to Changsha and take the 3-hour high-speed rail — it's often cheaper and more reliable than a direct flight.

## Getting Around the Forest Park

The Zhangjiajie National Forest Park is huge — the shuttle system is essential:

- **Shuttle buses**: Connect the main areas — Yuanjiajie (袁家界), Tianzi Mountain (天子山), and Golden Whip Stream (金鞭溪). Included in the ¥228 ticket.
- **Bailong Elevator (百龙天梯)**: 326 meters in 1 minute — the fastest way up the mountain. Worth it to save energy.
- **Cable cars**: Tianzi Mountain and Yangjiajie have cable cars — use them to save your legs.
- **Tip**: You'll walk 20,000+ steps a day — use the elevators and cable cars where you can.

## Where to Stay: Wulingyuan (武陵源)

- **The only sensible choice**: Wulingyuan is right at the park entrance — you can walk to the gates.
- **The city center is 40 minutes away**: Don't make that commute daily — it eats 1.5 hours of your day.
- **Tip**: Book a hotel near the Wulingyuan ticket gate, not the bus station.

## The Fenghuang Side Trip

- **High-speed rail**: 1 hour from Zhangjiajie West Station to Fenghuang.
- **The town**: Stilted houses (吊脚楼) along the Tuo River — stunning at night. It's commercialized, but still worth the trip.
- **Tip**: Go for the night views — the town is most magical after dark.

## Taxis & Didi

- **Flag-fall**: About ¥10 in Zhangjiajie city.
- **Didi**: Works in the city and Wulingyuan — but coverage is patchy in the mountains.
- **Tip**: For early-morning park entry, book a Didi the night before.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Golden Week**: Avoid early October — the queues are brutal. April–June and September–November are the sweet spots.
- **Monkeys**: The macaques in Golden Whip Stream grab bags and food — keep your backpack zipped.
- **Weather**: Mist and rain are the norm — the peaks look best right after rain. Pack a rain jacket.

## FAQ

**How do I get to Zhangjiajie?** Hehua Airport (few flights) or fly to Changsha + 3-hour high-speed rail.

**What is the best way to get around the Forest Park?** Shuttle buses + the Bailong Elevator and cable cars.

**Where should I stay?** Wulingyuan — right at the park entrance.

**How do I get to Fenghuang?** 1 hour by high-speed rail from Zhangjiajie West Station.

**Is the glass bridge worth it?** Yes — but skip it if you're afraid of heights.
