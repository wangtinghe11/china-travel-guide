# How to Get Around Guilin & Yangshuo: Trains, E-Bikes & the Li River

**The short answer**: take the Li River cruise from Guilin to Yangshuo, then rent an e-bike (¥30–50/day) to explore the countryside. The high-speed rail connects the region, but the last leg to Yangshuo town is a 40-minute car ride. Budget ¥20–50 for transport per day.

## Getting to Guilin

- **Liangjiang Airport (KWL)**: About 30 km from the city — the **airport bus costs ¥20**. Taxis/Didi are ¥80–120.
- **High-speed rail**: Guilin is well-connected — Guilin North and Guilin West stations serve the high-speed lines from Guangzhou, Shenzhen, Changsha, and beyond.
- **Tip**: Check which station your train arrives at — Guilin North is further from the city center than Guilin West.

## Guilin to Yangshuo: The Classic Route

- **Li River cruise (漓江游船)**: 4 hours from Guilin to Yangshuo — the classic way to see the karst peaks. Book in advance.
- **Bamboo raft (竹筏)**: On the essence section (精华段) — quieter and more intimate, but shorter.
- **High-speed rail**: 30 minutes from Guilin North/West to **Yangshuo station** — but the station is still **40 minutes by car** from the town itself. Factor that in.
- **Bus**: Direct buses from Guilin to Yangshuo take about 1.5 hours.

## Yangshuo: E-Bikes Are the Way

Yangshuo's countryside is the real attraction — and the best way to explore it is by e-bike.

- **Rental**: ¥30–50/day from shops in town.
- **The Ten-Mile Gallery (十里画廊)**: The classic ride — karst peaks, rice paddies, and villages.
- **Yulong River (遇龙河)**: A quieter, more beautiful ride along the river.
- **Tip**: Wear a helmet and watch for tour buses on the main roads.

## Getting to the Longji Rice Terraces

- **Distance**: About 2 hours from Guilin by bus or private car.
- **Best time**: May–June (flooded terraces, mirror effect) or September–October (golden harvest).
- **Stay overnight**: A village guesthouse lets you catch the sunrise — it's worth it.

## Taxis & Didi

- **Flag-fall**: About ¥10 in Guilin.
- **Didi**: Works in Guilin city and Yangshuo town — but coverage is patchy in the countryside.
- **Tip**: For the Longji terraces, book a private car or join a day tour — public transport is limited.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Yangshuo station distance**: The high-speed rail station is 40 minutes from the town — don't plan a tight connection.
- **Cash**: Small villages and farm restaurants may not take cards — carry some cash.
- **Rainy season**: June–August the Li River runs muddy — the water is clearest September–November.

## FAQ

**What is the best way to get around Guilin and Yangshuo?** The Li River cruise, then e-bikes in Yangshuo.

**How do I get from Guilin airport to the city?** Airport bus ¥20, about 30 km.

**How do I get from Guilin to Yangshuo?** Li River cruise (4 hours) or high-speed rail (30 min + 40 min car ride).

**Is the Li River cruise worth it?** Yes — the classic way to see the karst peaks.

**How do I get to the Longji Rice Terraces?** About 2 hours from Guilin — stay overnight for the sunrise.
