# Chongqing Travel Guide 2026: Itineraries, Transport & Insider Tips

Chongqing is China's most surreal city — a vertical, neon-lit mountain metropolis where the metro runs through apartment buildings, and hotpot is a religion. Here's how to do it right.

## Day 1: The 8D City

**Morning**: Start at **Liziba Station (李子坝)** — the metro that runs through a residential building. The viewing platform across the street is the classic photo spot. Then ride Line 2 along the river — the most scenic metro line in China.

**Afternoon**: **Jiefangbei (解放碑)** — the city's shopping heart — then walk to **Baixiangju (白象居)**, a 1990s residential complex with the best free photo spot of the cableway passing between buildings. It's also where *Better Days* (《少年的你》) was filmed — a favorite spot for movie fans.

**Evening**: **Hongya Cave (洪崖洞)** — but don't go inside. Cross **Qiansimen Bridge (千厮门大桥)** for the full view of the 11-story stilted complex glowing at night. It's the real-life Spirited Away scene.

## Day 2: River, Old Streets & Hotpot

**Morning**: **Yangtze River Cableway (长江索道)** — go early to beat the queue. The 5-minute crossing gives you a bird's-eye view of the two rivers meeting.

**Afternoon**: Walk the **Shancheng Trail (山城步道)** — a cliffside path built along the mountain, where you can experience old Chongqing with far fewer crowds. Then head to **Eling Second Factory (鹅岭二厂)** — a converted industrial park of art studios and cafés, the filming location of *I Belonged to You* (《从你的全世界路过》). Or visit **Ciqikou (磁器口)** old town if you prefer the classic tourist lane.

**Evening**: **Hotpot night** — the city's religion. Order the **nine-grid pot (九宫格)** with beef tallow, and remember: even "mild" here is spicy. Try **xiaomian (小面)** — the classic breakfast noodle, ¥7–10 for two liang, with **wanzamian (豌杂面)** the signature version.

## Day 3 (Optional): Wulong Karst

**Wulong (武隆)** — the **Three Natural Bridges (天生三桥)** and Furong Cave — is a stunning day trip, about 2.5 hours by car (or 2 hours by high-speed rail). This is where parts of *Transformers* and *The Curse of the Golden Flower* were filmed.

## Where to Eat Like a Local

- **Nine-grid hotpot (九宫格)**: The classic Chongqing pot — beef tallow, numbing spice, and nine compartments for different cooking times.
- **Xiaomian (小面)**: The humble breakfast noodle — ¥7–10, and the wanzamian (pea-and-pork) version is the classic.
- **Suānlàfěn (酸辣粉)**: Hot-and-sour sweet potato noodles — try the famous **Haoyoulai (好又来)** stall near Jiefangbei, about ¥10 a bowl.
- **Jianghu cuisine (江湖菜)**: Bold, rustic Sichuan-style dishes — the city's answer to fine dining.
- **Night markets**: Jiefangbei and Guanyinqiao have great street food after dark.

## Practical Tips

- **Navigation**: GPS is unreliable in this vertical city — the metro is the only sane way around. Wear comfortable shoes: everything is slopes and stairs.
- **Airport**: Jiangbei Airport (CKG) connects to downtown via Metro Line 10 in about 40 minutes.
- **Taxis**: The "yellow Ferrari" taxis are famously aggressive drivers — if you get carsick easily, take the metro instead.
- **Cableway**: The queue is often 1 hour for a 5-minute ride. Go at dusk for the best light, or skip it and photograph it from Baixiangju.
- **Weather**: Hot and humid in summer (it's one of China's "furnace" cities, 40°C+); damp and cold in winter, with heavy fog — hence "Fog Capital" (雾都). May and October are the sweet spot.
- **Language**: English is limited — have a translation app ready.

## Where to Stay

- **Jiefangbei / Hongya Cave area** — central, nightlife, near the top sights. When booking a "river-view" room, check the photos carefully — some are blocked by trees.
- **Nan'an** — skyline views across the river
- **Guanyinqiao** — shopping district, local life
