# What to Eat in Zhangjiajie: Sanxiaguo, Tujia Bacon & Mountain Food

**The short answer**: eat sanxiaguo (三下锅) — a hotpot of three meats — in Wulingyuan town, try Tujia bacon (土家腊肉), and snack on kudzu root noodles (葛根粉). Hunan food is famously spicy, so be ready. Budget ¥50–80 per person.

## The Must-Try List

### 1. Sanxiaguo (三下锅) — the local specialty
A Zhangjiajie hotpot of three meats — usually pork, beef, and chicken — cooked with chilies, garlic, and spices in a bubbling pot. It's the dish to try, and it comes with a serious chili kick. **Eat it in town, not inside the scenic area** — the restaurants at the park are overpriced and average.

- **Where**: Wulingyuan town or Zhangjiajie city — look for the busiest shop.
- **Price**: ¥50–80 per person.

### 2. Tujia Bacon (土家腊肉) — smoked mountain pork
Smoked pork from the Tujia minority villages — rich, savory, and slightly smoky. Often served stir-fried with mountain vegetables or in the sanxiaguo.

- **Where**: Local restaurants in Wulingyuan and the Tujia villages.
- **Price**: ¥30–50 per dish.

### 3. Kudzu Root Noodles (葛根粉) — the mountain specialty
A local specialty made from wild kudzu root — chewy noodles or a jelly-like dessert. Refreshing and unique to the region.

- **Where**: Street stalls and local restaurants.
- **Price**: ¥10–20.

### 4. Wild Mountain Vegetables (山野菜)
The park area is famous for foraged greens — bamboo shoots, fiddlehead ferns, and other wild vegetables you won't find in the city. Fresh, earthy, and delicious.

- **Where**: Local restaurants in Wulingyuan.
- **Price**: ¥20–40 per dish.

### 5. Fenghuang Snacks (if you go)
If you take the side trip to Fenghuang Ancient Town, try the stilted-house riverside snacks: rice tofu (米豆腐), blood sausage (血粑鸭), and ginger candy (姜糖).

## Local vs Tourist: Where to Eat

- **Skip**: Restaurants inside the scenic area — overpriced and average.
- **Go**: Wulingyuan town and Zhangjiajie city — the busiest shops are the best.
- **Rule of thumb**: If the menu is in Chinese and the seats are plastic, you're in the right place.

## Street Food vs Sit-Down

- **Street food (¥10–30)**: Kudzu root noodles, grilled skewers, and mountain snacks.
- **Sit-down (¥50–80)**: Sanxiaguo and Tujia bacon — the two signature experiences.

## Spice Warning

- **Hunan is the spiciest province in China** — and Zhangjiajie is no exception. Sanxiaguo comes with a serious chili kick.
- Order **"not spicy" (不辣)** if you can't handle heat — but be aware that even "not spicy" in Hunan has some chili.
- Keep **yogurt or soy milk** on the table to cut the heat.

## Practical Tips

- **Eat in town**: The scenic area restaurants are overpriced — eat in Wulingyuan or the city.
- **No tipping**: Tipping is not expected.
- **Payment**: Alipay and WeChat Pay work everywhere; carry a little cash for small stalls.
