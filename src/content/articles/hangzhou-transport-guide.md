# How to Get Around Hangzhou: West Lake Cycling, Metro & Day Trips

**The short answer**: cycle around West Lake — it's the classic Hangzhou experience — and use the metro for everything else. The 15 km lake loop is best done by bike, early in the morning when it's quiet. Budget ¥2–8 per metro ride, ¥10–20 for a shared bike.

## Cycling: The West Lake Way

Hangzhou is one of China's most bike-friendly cities, and cycling the West Lake loop is the classic experience.

- **The loop**: 15 km around the lake, taking 1.5–2 hours with stops. Go early (before 8 AM) when the mist is on the water and the crowds haven't arrived.
- **Bike-sharing**: Shared bikes (Alipay/WeChat Pay) are everywhere — scan and ride, about ¥1.5 per 30 minutes.
- **Best stops**: Su Causeway (苏堤), Bai Causeway (白堤), and the sunset from Baoshi Hill (宝石山).

## The Metro: Clean & Efficient

Hangzhou's metro is modern and English-signed.

- **Lines you'll use most**: Line 1 (West Lake, Wulin Square, Xiaoshan Airport), Line 2 (West Lake, Longjing area), Line 19 (airport express).
- **Payment**: Scan a QR code with Alipay or WeChat Pay at the gates — no ticket needed.
- **Fares**: ¥2–8 per ride.
- **Peak hours**: 7:30–9:30 AM and 5:30–7:30 PM.

## Airport Transfer: Xiaoshan Airport (HGH)

- **Metro Lines 1 & 19**: From Xiaoshan Airport to downtown in about 40 minutes — the best option.
- **Taxi/Didi**: 40–60 minutes depending on traffic, ¥80–120.
- **Tip**: Line 19 is the airport express — faster and more comfortable.

## Day Trips: The Yangtze Delta Hub

Hangzhou is perfectly positioned for day trips:

- **Shanghai (45 min by high-speed rail)**: The Bund, the skyline, the shopping.
- **Suzhou (1 hour)**: Classical gardens and canals.
- **Wuzhen / Xitang (1–1.5 hours)**: Ancient water towns.
- **Qiandao Lake (2 hours)**: A stunning reservoir with thousands of islands.

## Taxis & Didi

- **Flag-fall**: About ¥12.
- **Didi**: Works well — set your pickup point carefully.
- **Tip**: The metro is almost always faster in rush hour.

## The Qianjiang New City Light Show (钱江新城灯光秀)

A spectacular light show on the Qianjiang New City skyline — runs at **19:30 and 20:30**, and it's **free** to watch from the **City Balcony (城市阳台)**. A lovely evening activity after a West Lake day.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Cashless city**: Hangzhou is Alipay's home city — the whole city is cashless. Make sure your Alipay is set up.
- **Summer heat**: 35°C+ in summer — the metro is air-conditioned, and cycling is best in the early morning.
- **Winter dampness**: Winter is damp and cold — dress in layers.

## FAQ

**What is the best way to get around Hangzhou?** Cycling around West Lake, and the metro for everything else.

**How do I get from Xiaoshan Airport to the city?** Metro Lines 1 and 19, about 40 minutes.

**Is West Lake really free?** Yes — the entire lake area is free. The 15 km loop is best by bike.

**Can I do a day trip to Shanghai?** Yes — 45 minutes by high-speed rail. Suzhou is about 1 hour.

**Is the Qianjiang light show worth it?** Yes — 19:30 and 20:30, free from the City Balcony.
