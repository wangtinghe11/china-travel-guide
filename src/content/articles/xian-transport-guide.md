# How to Get Around Xi'an: Metro, Airport & the City Wall

**The short answer**: take the metro — it covers all major sights and is cheap. For the airport, Metro Line 14 goes direct. Taxis are cheap (¥8.5 flag-fall) but traffic jams at peak hours make the metro faster.

## The Metro: Your Best Friend

Xi'an's metro is modern, cheap, and English-signed — it covers all the major sights.

- **Lines you'll use most**: Line 2 (Bell Tower, city wall, Muslim Quarter), Line 3 (Big Wild Goose Pagoda, Qujiang), Line 4 (Daming Palace, railway station), Line 14 (airport).
- **Payment**: Scan Alipay's or WeChat Pay's transport QR code at the gates.
- **Fares**: ¥2–8 per ride.
- **Peak hours**: 7:30–9:30 AM and 5:30–7:30 PM — packed.

## Airport Transfer: Xianyang Airport (XIY)

Xianyang Airport is about **40 km** from the city — not close, but well connected.

- **Metro Line 14**: Connects the airport directly to downtown — the best option.
- **Airport bus**: ¥25 — a budget alternative to the metro.
- **Taxi/Didi**: 40–60 minutes depending on traffic, ¥100–150.

## Taxis & Didi

- **Flag-fall**: Only **¥8.5** — short trips are very affordable.
- **Didi (滴滴)**: Shows the fare upfront — more transparent. Set your pickup point carefully.
- **Warning**: Traffic jams at peak hours are brutal — metro first, taxi second.

## The City Wall: Cycle the Old City

The best way to see Xi'an's old city: rent a bike at the city wall gates and cycle the full **13.7 km** perimeter. It takes about 1.5–2 hours with stops.

- **Best time**: Sunset — the golden hour over the old city is spectacular.
- **Cost**: Bike rental ~¥45 for 3 hours; wall entry ¥54.

## Day Trips

- **Mount Hua (华山)**: 2 hours by high-speed rail — the famous Plank Walk in the Sky. Take the cable car if you're not fit.
- **Terracotta Army**: Tourist bus (Line 5/306) or a Didi (~¥150), about 1 hour.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Peak-hour traffic**: The city center jams up badly — the metro is almost always faster.
- **Airport distance**: Xianyang is 40 km out — don't plan a tight connection.
- **City wall bikes**: Rent at the gates, not from street vendors — the official rental is cheaper and better maintained.

## FAQ

**How do I get from the airport?** Metro Line 14 direct, or the ¥25 airport bus.

**Is the metro good?** Yes — covers all major sights, cheap and English-signed.

**Are taxis cheap?** Yes — ¥8.5 flag-fall, but avoid peak hours.

**Can I cycle the city wall?** Yes — 13.7 km, about 1.5–2 hours, sunset is best.

**How do I get to Mount Hua?** 2 hours by high-speed rail.
