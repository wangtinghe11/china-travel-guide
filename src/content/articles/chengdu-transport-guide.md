# How to Get Around Chengdu: Metro, Airports & Day Trips

**The short answer**: take the metro — it's excellent, cheap, and English-signed. Don't mix up the two airports: Tianfu is far (1 hour by metro), Shuangliu is close. For day trips, high-speed rail reaches Dujiangyan in 30 minutes.

## Airports: Don't Mix Them Up!

Chengdu has two airports — and they're on opposite sides of the city.

- **Tianfu Airport (TFU)**: Far from the city — about **1 hour by metro** (Line 18). Most international flights land here.
- **Shuangliu Airport (CTU)**: Much **closer to the city**. Mostly domestic flights.
- **Warning**: Check which airport your flight uses before booking a hotel — they're 50+ km apart!

## The Metro: Your Best Friend

- **Coverage**: 10+ lines covering all major sights — Panda Base, Kuanzhai Alley, People's Park, Chunxi Road.
- **Payment**: Scan the QR code at the gates with Alipay or WeChat Pay.
- **Fares**: ¥2–8 per ride.
- **Panda Base**: Take the metro then the shuttle bus — the base is outside the city center.
- **Peak hours**: 7:30–9:30 AM and 5:30–7:30 PM — packed.

## Taxis & Didi

- **Taxis**: Metered and reasonable. Airport runs cost ¥80–150 depending on distance.
- **Didi (滴滴)**: Shows the fare upfront — more transparent. Set your pickup point carefully.
- **Tip**: In rush hour, the metro is almost always faster.

## Day Trips by High-Speed Rail

Chengdu is the perfect base for day trips:

- **Dujiangyan**: 30 minutes — the 2,000-year-old irrigation system, genuinely awe-inspiring. Combine with Mount Qingcheng.
- **Leshan**: About 1 hour — the giant Buddha.
- **Skip the far-flung parks**: Jiuzhaigou and Daocheng Yading are stunning but a multi-day trip. With only 3–4 days, spend them on Dujiangyan and Leshan.

Book tickets on the official 12306 app or at the station with your passport.

## Nightlife: Jiuyanqiao

**Jiuyanqiao (九眼桥)** bar street gets lively at night — a fun spot for a drink after dinner. The metro runs until about 11 PM; after that, use Didi.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Wrong airport**: TFU vs CTU — they're 50+ km apart. Always check.
- **Rush hour**: The metro is brutally packed at peak times.
- **Weather**: Chengdu is cloudy and rains often — carry an umbrella, and factor rain into your transport plans.

## FAQ

**Which airport should I fly into?** Shuangliu (CTU) is close; Tianfu (TFU) is 1 hour by metro. Check your ticket!

**How do I get from Tianfu Airport?** Metro Line 18, about 1 hour.

**Is the metro good?** Yes — excellent, cheap, English-signed. QR code entry.

**Can I do a day trip?** Yes — Dujiangyan in 30 minutes, Leshan in 1 hour.

**What's the nightlife like?** Jiuyanqiao bar street — lively until late.
