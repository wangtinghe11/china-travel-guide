# Shanghai Travel Guide 2026: 3-Day Itinerary & First-Timer Tips

Shanghai is China's most international city — a dazzling mix of colonial architecture, futuristic skyscrapers, and world-class food. Here's the perfect 3-day plan.

## Day 1: The Skyline

**Morning**: **The Bund** (colonial waterfront, walk the promenade) → **Nanjing Road** (China's most famous shopping street)

**Afternoon**: Cross to **Pudong** — Shanghai Tower (632 m, fastest elevator in the world), Oriental Pearl, Lujiazui skyline walk

**Evening**: **Huangpu River night cruise** (or the ¥2 ferry for budget views) — the skyline at night is unforgettable. The Bund lights up at 7 PM in summer, 5:30 PM in winter — the half hour after dark is the most beautiful.

## Day 2: The French Concession

**Morning**: **Wukang Road** (the most beautiful street in Shanghai, with the iconic Wukang Mansion photo spot) → **Former French Concession** cafés and boutiques

**Afternoon**: **Tianzifang** (artsy lanes, craft shops) → **Xintiandi** (restored shikumen houses, upscale dining)

**Evening**: Dinner in the Concession — book a popular spot via Dianping. Rent a shared bike to cruise the tree-lined streets at dusk.

## Day 3: Old & New

**Morning**: **Yu Garden** (Ming dynasty classical garden, arrive early) → **City God Temple** bazaar

**Afternoon**: **Shanghai Museum** (free, world-class bronzes) or **M50** (contemporary art district)

**Evening**: Last dinner — xiaolongbao (soup dumplings) at a local favorite

## Where to Eat Like a Local

- **Benbang (本帮) cuisine** is sweet — locals love **Lao Jishi (老吉士)** and **Lanxin (兰心)**.
- **Shengjian (生煎)**: Order one liang (4 pieces) — don't over-order. **Da Huchun (大壶春)** is the traditional fermented-dough school.
- **Xiaolongbao**: Soup dumplings are a must — go where the queue is, it's usually worth it.

## Practical Tips

- **Metro**: Excellent and English-signed. The Maglev from Pudong Airport is worth the ¥50 — 8 minutes to Longyang Road.
- **Ferry**: The ¥2 Huangpu ferry is the best budget skyline view — take the Dongchang Road line at sunset.
- **Airports**: **Hongqiao (SHA)** is close to the city. **Pudong (PVG)** is far east — Maglev or Metro Line 2. Don't book a hotel near the wrong one!
- **Weather**: Spring and autumn are best. The plum rain season (mid-June to early July) is muggy; winter is damp and cold.
- **Day trips**: High-speed rail reaches **Suzhou and Hangzhou in 30 minutes** — easy to add to your itinerary.
- **Language**: English is more common here than anywhere else in China, but a translation app still helps.

## Where to Stay

- **The Bund / People's Square** — central, classic
- **Former French Concession** — charming, cafés
- **Lujiazui** — modern, skyline views
