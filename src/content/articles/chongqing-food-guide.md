# What to Eat in Chongqing: Must-Try Dishes & Where to Find Them

**The short answer**: eat nine-grid hotpot (九宫格) with beef tallow, xiaomian (小面) noodles for breakfast, and suanlafen (酸辣粉) from the Haoyoulai stall near Jiefangbei. Go where locals queue — the busier the shop, the better the food. Budget ¥60–100 per person for hotpot, ¥10–20 for street food.

## The Must-Try List

### 1. Nine-Grid Hotpot (九宫格火锅) — the city's religion
The classic Chongqing pot: a nine-compartment iron pot filled with bubbling beef tallow, dried chilies, and Sichuan peppercorns. Different compartments cook different things — the center boils hardest for quick-cooking meats, the outer rings simmer for slow-cooked items. Order **maodu (毛肚, beef tripe)**, **huanghou (黄喉, pork aorta)**, and **yaohua (腰花, kidney)** — the holy trinity of Chongqing hotpot.

- **Where**: Any busy hotpot street. Jiefangbei and Guanyinqiao have dozens. Look for queues of locals.
- **Price**: ¥60–100 per person.
- **Spice warning**: Even "mild" (微辣) here is like medium-spicy elsewhere. Order a **yuanyang pot (鸳鸯锅)** — half spicy, half clear broth — if you're not a veteran.

### 2. Xiaomian (小面) — the breakfast of champions
The humble breakfast noodle: wheat noodles in a fiery broth of chili oil, Sichuan pepper, garlic, and scallions. **Wanzamian (豌杂面)** — topped with soft peas and minced pork — is the signature version. Two liang (二两) is the standard order.

- **Where**: Any small noodle shop. The best ones are tiny, grubby, and packed at 8 AM.
- **Price**: ¥7–10.

### 3. Suanlafen (酸辣粉) — hot-and-sour sweet potato noodles
Chewy sweet-potato noodles in a sour, spicy, numbing broth with peanuts and pickled vegetables. The famous **Haoyoulai (好又来)** stall near Jiefangbei has been serving it for decades.

- **Where**: Haoyoulai (好又来), Jiefangbei — look for the long queue.
- **Price**: About ¥10 a bowl.

### 4. Jianghu Cuisine (江湖菜) — bold, rustic, addictive
"Rivers-and-lakes" cooking: big flavors, heavy oil, lots of chili. Think twice-fried pork, spicy chicken with dried chilies (辣子鸡), and boiled fish in chili oil (水煮鱼). This is Chongqing's answer to fine dining — loud, generous, and unforgettable.

- **Where**: Local restaurants across the city; ask your hotel for a nearby jianghu place.
- **Price**: ¥50–80 per person.

### 5. Night Market Snacks
Jiefangbei and Guanyinqiao come alive after dark with skewers, grilled eggplant, and stinky tofu. Just follow the smoke and the crowds.

## Local vs Tourist: Where to Eat

- **Skip**: Restaurants inside Hongya Cave and the main tourist strips — overpriced and tuned for one-time visitors.
- **Go**: Jiefangbei side streets, Guanyinqiao, and any shop with a queue of locals. In Chongqing, a long line is the best quality signal.
- **Rule of thumb**: If the menu is only in Chinese and the seats are plastic, you're in the right place.

## Street Food vs Sit-Down

- **Street food (¥5–20)**: Xiaomian, suanlafen, grilled skewers, and the famous **chuanchuan (串串)** — skewers cooked in a shared hotpot broth, charged by the stick.
- **Sit-down (¥50–100)**: Hotpot and jianghu cuisine. Hotpot is a social event — go with friends, order more than you think, and let the beef tallow work its magic.

## Spice Survival Guide (for foreigners)

- **微辣 (weila, "mild")** in Chongqing = medium-spicy elsewhere. **中辣 (zhongla)** = very spicy. **特辣 (tela)** = don't.
- Order a **yuanyang pot** if you're not a chili veteran.
- Keep **yogurt, soy milk, or beer** on the table — dairy and cold drinks cut the heat better than water.
- The numbing (麻) from Sichuan peppercorn is different from the burn — you'll feel your lips tingle. That's normal, and it's the point.

## FAQ

**What food is Chongqing famous for?** Nine-grid hotpot with beef tallow is the city's religion, followed by xiaomian noodles and suanlafen.

**Is Chongqing food too spicy for foreigners?** It's the spiciest food city in China. Even "mild" is like medium-spicy elsewhere. Order a yuanyang pot and keep yogurt or soy milk handy.

**How much does food cost?** Street food ¥10–20, hotpot ¥60–100 per person, jianghu cuisine ¥50–80 per person.

**Where do locals eat?** Jiefangbei side streets, Guanyinqiao, and any busy-looking small shop. The busier, the better.

**What's the best breakfast?** Xiaomian — ¥7–10 for two liang, with wanzamian the signature version.
