# How to Get Around Shanghai: Metro, Maglev & Airports

**The short answer**: take the metro — it's excellent and English-signed. For Pudong Airport, ride the Maglev (8 minutes, world's fastest). Don't mix up the two airports, and use the ¥2 Huangpu ferry for the best budget skyline views.

## Airports: Don't Mix Them Up!

Shanghai has two airports — and they're far apart.

- **Pudong (PVG)**: Handles most **international** flights. Far east of the city — Maglev (8 min to Longyang Road, ¥50) or Metro Line 2.
- **Hongqiao (SHA)**: Much **closer to the city** — about 30 minutes by metro. Mostly domestic flights.
- **Warning**: Don't book a hotel near the wrong one! Check your arrival airport before booking.

## The Maglev: World's Fastest Train

The Shanghai Maglev hits **431 km/h** — the fastest commercial train on Earth. It connects Pudong Airport to Longyang Road in **8 minutes** (¥50 one way). Worth doing at least once, even if the metro is cheaper.

## The Metro: Your Best Friend

- **Coverage**: 20+ lines covering every major sight — Bund, French Concession, Yu Garden, Disneyland.
- **Payment**: Scan Alipay's or WeChat Pay's transport QR code at the gates, or use a transit card.
- **Fares**: ¥3–10 per ride.
- **Peak hours**: 7:30–9:30 AM and 5:30–7:30 PM — packed.

## The ¥2 Huangpu Ferry

The best budget skyline view in Shanghai: the ferry across the Huangpu River costs **¥2**. Take the Dongchang Road line at sunset — the Bund and Pudong skyline from the water is unforgettable.

## Shared Bikes: The French Concession Way

Shanghai is flat and bike-friendly — shared bikes are everywhere. Rent one and cruise the tree-lined streets of the **French Concession** at dusk. Scan with Alipay or WeChat.

## Taxis & Didi

- **Taxis**: Metered and reasonable. Airport runs cost ¥150–250 depending on distance and traffic.
- **Didi (滴滴)**: Shows the fare upfront — more transparent. Set your pickup point carefully.
- **Tip**: In rush hour, the metro is almost always faster.

## Day Trips by High-Speed Rail

Shanghai is the perfect base for day trips:

- **Suzhou**: 30 minutes — classical gardens and canals.
- **Hangzhou**: 30 minutes — West Lake.
- **Nanjing**: About 1 hour — Ming dynasty history.

Book tickets on the official 12306 app or at the station with your passport.

## Walking: The Suzhou Creek Route

For a quiet, scenic walk: follow the **Suzhou Creek (苏州河) waterfront** from the Bund to the **M50 art district** — fewer crowds than the Bund promenade, with great views of old industrial Shanghai.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Wrong airport**: PVG vs SHA — they're 40+ km apart. Always check.
- **Rush hour**: The metro is brutally packed at peak times.
- **Maglev vs metro**: The Maglev only goes to Longyang Road — you still need the metro from there. For direct trips, Metro Line 2 is simpler.

## FAQ

**Which airport should I fly into?** Pudong for international, Hongqiao for domestic — Hongqiao is much closer to the city.

**Is the Maglev worth it?** Yes — 431 km/h, 8 minutes from Pudong Airport. Do it once.

**How do I get from Pudong Airport to the city?** Maglev + metro, or Metro Line 2 directly.

**How do I pay for the metro?** Alipay/WeChat transport QR code or transit card.

**Can I do a day trip?** Yes — Suzhou and Hangzhou in 30 minutes, Nanjing in 1 hour.

**How do I get a cheap SIM?** Carrier counters at Pudong Airport sell tourist SIMs with your passport — cheaper than eSIM but more hassle.
