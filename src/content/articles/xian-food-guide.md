# What to Eat in Xi'an: Noodles, Roujiamo & Muslim Quarter Food

**The short answer**: eat biangbiang noodles and roujiamo from any busy small shop, yangrou paomo for a hearty meal, and skip the main Muslim Quarter strip — locals eat at Sajin Bridge and Da Piyuan. Budget ¥80–120 per day.

## The Must-Try List

### 1. Biangbiang Noodles (裤带面) — the belt noodles
Hand-pulled noodles as wide as a belt, chewy and satisfying, tossed in chili oil, garlic, and vinegar. The name's character has 58 strokes — the most complex in Chinese.

- **Where**: Any small local joint beats the tourist spots — the simpler the shop, the better the noodles.
- **Price**: ¥15–25.

### 2. Roujiamo (肉夹馍) — the "Chinese burger"
Crispy flatbread stuffed with slow-braised pork, cilantro, and chili. Grab one from a street stall — it's the ultimate grab-and-go.

- **Where**: Street stalls across the city; the Muslim Quarter's side lanes do it well.
- **Price**: ¥8–15.

### 3. Yangrou Paomo (羊肉泡馍) — lamb soup with torn bread
A Xi'an institution: you tear flatbread into tiny pieces, the kitchen cooks it with lamb broth, and you eat it with pickled garlic and chili paste. Hearty, warming, and filling.

- **Where**: Famous paomo houses in the Muslim Quarter — look for queues of locals.
- **Price**: ¥30–50.

### 4. Liangpi (凉皮) — cold noodles
Cold rice or wheat noodles in a tangy chili-vinegar dressing with cucumber and bean sprouts. The perfect summer dish.

- **Where**: Any busy noodle shop; often sold at roujiamo stalls.
- **Price**: ¥8–15.

### 5. The Muslim Quarter Snack Crawl
- **Suanmeitang (酸梅汤)**: Sour plum drink — refreshing in summer.
- **Bingfeng (冰峰)**: The local orange soda — Xi'an's answer to Coca-Cola, served with almost every meal.
- **Where**: Sajin Bridge (洒金桥) and Da Piyuan (大皮院) — cleaner, cheaper, and more local than the main strip.

## Local vs Tourist: Where to Eat

- **Skip**: The main Huimin Street (回民街) strip — tourist-priced and crowded.
- **Go**: **Sajin Bridge (洒金桥)** and **Da Piyuan (大皮院)** — smaller lanes with better food and lower prices. **Yongxingfang (永兴坊)** is the birthplace of the bowl-smashing wine trend — cleaner than the main strip, and locals go too.
- **Rule of thumb**: If the menu is only in Chinese and the seats are plastic, you're in the right place.

## Hanfu & Food Photos

Rent a **hanfu (traditional dress)** — ¥100–300 for a full outfit and photos — and eat your way through the old city in style. The city wall and Big Wild Goose Pagoda are the best backdrops.

## FAQ

**What food is Xi'an famous for?** Biangbiang noodles, roujiamo, yangrou paomo, and liangpi. Drink Bingfeng soda.

**Where do locals eat?** Sajin Bridge and Da Piyuan, not the main Huimin Street strip.

**What is biangbiang noodle?** A wide, chewy hand-pulled noodle in chili oil, garlic, and vinegar.

**What is yangrou paomo?** Lamb soup with torn flatbread — you tear the bread, the kitchen cooks it.

**How much does food cost?** Street food ¥10–30, full meals ¥30–60 per person.

**What's the local drink?** Bingfeng orange soda — the Xi'an staple.
