Who we are

{{ site.name }} is an independent travel guide focused on China's visa-free cities. We write for Western travelers who want practical, honest, up-to-date information — not recycled brochure copy.

How we research

  • On-the-ground experience: our writers visit the cities we cover and update guides after each trip.
  • Official sources: visa policy, train schedules, and entry rules are checked against government sources.
  • Regular updates: every guide carries a "last updated" date. Visa rules and prices change — we track them.

Editorial standards

  • We disclose affiliate links and sponsored content clearly.
  • We correct errors promptly — tell us if you spot one.
  • We never accept payment for positive coverage.
