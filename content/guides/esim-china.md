# eSIM & Internet in China: Stay Connected Without a VPN Headache

Here's the honest truth: **Google, Gmail, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, and many Western news sites are blocked in China.** But staying connected is easier than ever — an eSIM with a foreign carrier routes your traffic outside the firewall, so your apps just work. This guide covers the options.

## The three ways to get online

### Option 1: eSIM (recommended for most travelers)

An eSIM from a foreign provider (Airalo, Holafly, Nomad, Saily, etc.) gives you data on China's networks while routing through servers outside China — which means **Google, WhatsApp, and Instagram work normally**.

1. Buy an eSIM plan for China before you travel (Airalo, Holafly, Nomad all sell China plans).
2. Install it on your phone (scan a QR code — takes 2 minutes).
3. Activate when you land. Done.

**Pros:** works immediately, no VPN needed, apps all work. **Cons:** slightly more expensive than a local SIM, and you get a foreign IP (some Chinese apps may behave differently).

### Option 2: Local SIM card (cheapest data)

China Mobile, China Unicom, and China Telecom sell tourist SIMs at airports. Data is cheap, but **the Great Firewall applies** — Google and WhatsApp won't work without a VPN.

**Pros:** cheap, fast, local number (useful for Didi and food delivery). **Cons:** need a VPN for Western apps.

### Option 3: Roaming with your home carrier

Many carriers offer China roaming packages. Your home network's data works, and depending on your carrier, blocked apps may or may not work. Check with your carrier before you go.

## VPN: the essential backup

Even with an eSIM, keep a **VPN installed and tested before you arrive**:

- **Install and test at home** — the app store is accessible outside China; it may not be once you're in.
- **Good options:** ExpressVPN, NordVPN, Surfshark, Mullvad (check current status — some get blocked).
- **Free VPNs are unreliable** in China. Pay for a reputable one.
- **Download offline maps** (Google Maps offline, or use Amap/高德地图) as a backup.

## Apps that work without a VPN

- **WeChat** — essential for messaging locals and paying.
- **Alipay** — payments, metro, translation.
- **Didi** — ride-hailing (English version available).
- **Amap (高德地图)** — Chinese Google Maps, works well in English mode.
- **Trip.com** — hotels, trains, flights.
- **Metro apps** — most cities have English metro apps or Alipay transport QR codes.

## FAQ

**Q: Is Google Maps blocked in China?**
**A:** Yes, Google Maps doesn't work reliably. Use Amap (高德地图) or Apple Maps (which works in China) instead.

**Q: Will my eSIM work for calls?**
**A:** Most travel eSIMs are data-only. For calls, use WeChat voice or WhatsApp (with VPN/eSIM).

**Q: Do I need a VPN if I have an eSIM?**
**A:** No — a foreign eSIM routes traffic outside China, so blocked apps work. But keep a VPN as backup for hotel WiFi.

**Q: Is hotel WiFi fast?**
**A:** Usually yes, but the firewall applies. Use your eSIM for blocked apps.

**Q: Can I buy an eSIM in China?**
**A:** Yes, at airports and from Chinese carriers, but foreign eSIMs are easier to set up before you arrive.
